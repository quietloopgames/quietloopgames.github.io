Worst Shift Ever
Privacy Policy

iPhone game · Last updated September 27, 2026

Worst Shift Ever is an iPhone game by Quiet Loop Games. This policy describes how the game and the services it uses handle information.

Game progress

Your Tips, unlocked characters and cosmetics, upgrades, achievements, career totals, recent shifts, and game settings are saved on your device. The game has no developer-operated account system or cloud save. Removing the app or clearing its storage may erase this local progress.

Optional Game Center

You can play without connecting Game Center. If you connect, Apple may process your Game Center account identifier, two cumulative leaderboard scores (active survival time and customers served), and completed achievements to provide those features. Other players may see leaderboard information according to your Game Center profile settings. Game Center scores are not a backup of your local saved game.

Advertising and privacy choices

The free version can show a banner on the menu, an interstitial after a shift, and an optional rewarded ad when you choose to claim extra Tips. Google AdMob and its partners may process information such as IP address, device or app identifiers, general device information, ad interactions, and diagnostics to deliver, secure, and measure ads. Where required, the game presents an advertising consent form and a Privacy Options control to change your choices. The optional Supporter Pack removes banner and interstitial ads and provides daily Tips claims without watching rewarded ads.

Optional purchase

The Supporter Pack is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple's App Store. The game checks purchase entitlements to unlock and restore it. Apple handles your payment information; Quiet Loop Games does not receive your full card or bank details.

Permissions and third-party services

Core gameplay does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or precise location. Internet access is used by Game Center, the App Store, advertising, and consent services. Apple and Google process information according to their own policies:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

Your choices and retention

You may play without Game Center, choose whether to view rewarded ads, use the in-app Privacy Options control when available, or remove local game data by deleting the app. Apple's and Google's records are managed under their own policies and settings. The game's locally stored progress remains on the device until removed. Contact us if you have a privacy or deletion request concerning information controlled by Quiet Loop Games.

Children and policy updates

The game is intended for a general audience and does not include chat or player-to-player messaging. If you believe a child has shared personal information with us, please contact us. We may update this policy if the game or its services change; the last updated date above will then change.
