Privacy Policy

Quiet Loop Games · iOS and Android

Last updated 5 November 2026

Scope and contact

This policy describes how Quiet Loop Games handles information for Volcano Gem Forge on iOS and Android. Advertising and purchases apply where those features are enabled in your version.

For privacy questions or requests, contact quiteloopgames@gmail.com.

Game data on your device

Volcano Gem Forge stores your level progress, scores, gem balance, lives, unlocked items and settings on your device. Local records also keep track of ad timing and delivered purchase transactions. The game uses these records to resume play and apply rewards once.

Progress is stored locally. If you use a device backup, its provider may retain a copy under your backup settings.

Advertising and privacy choices

Versions with advertising use Google AdMob and Google's consent tools. Google and its participating advertising partners may process app or device identifiers, advertising identifiers where permitted, IP addresses used to estimate general location, ad and app interactions, consent choices, and performance or diagnostic information.

They use this information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and improve advertising performance. Ads may be personalised where allowed by your choices and platform settings.

Use Ad privacy in game Settings when available. You can also manage available advertising and tracking controls in your device settings. Learn more in Google's explanation of data from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Store purchases

Apple's App Store or Google Play processes purchases made through that store. The game uses product and transaction information from the store to verify delivery and prevent duplicate credits. Your store manages payment details, purchase history and refund requests under its own policies.

Display fonts

The game may request a font from Google Fonts. Google receives your IP address and standard request information to deliver the font. See Google Fonts privacy information.

Support, retention and deletion

If you email us, we use your email address, message and any information you include to respond and investigate your request. We retain correspondence for as long as necessary for support and applicable recordkeeping obligations.

Use Reset all progress in Settings or remove the app and its data to clear your local game save. Device backups and records held by app stores or ad providers are managed through those providers.

Google Mobile Ads sends SDK data over encrypted connections. Keep your device and backups secure to protect local game data.

Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information we handle, or to exercise other rights available where you live. You can change advertising choices through the controls described above. Our studio privacy policy explains more about these rights and how to contact us.
