Common questions

How do Daily Challenge rewards work?

Open Daily Challenge from the main menu. Your first victory each day earns 50 gems. Further wins that day earn the replay reward.

Where can I change the sound?

Open Settings to adjust music and effects. During a level, use Pause and open Sound settings.

Where is my game progress stored?

The game saves progress and settings on your device. Reset all progress clears the local game save.

Who can help with a purchase?

Email us for help with items in the game. Your app store manages payments and refunds.
