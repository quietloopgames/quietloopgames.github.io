1. About this policy

Quiet Loop Games (“we”, “us” or “our”) develops and publishes Prince from the Cauldron. This policy applies to the installed iOS and Android versions of the game. Quiet Loop Games is responsible for personal information that we handle for the purposes described here. Contact us at quiteloopgames@gmail.com.

You do not need a Quiet Loop Games account to play. Core gameplay works offline. Features may differ between platforms and releases; optional platform services described below process information only when those features are available and you use them.

2. No advertising or advertising tracking

Closed-test builds provide full access for free. The ten-character limit and Full Game Unlock purchase are not enabled in these test builds.

For the public release, the game is planned to remain free to download, with the first ten distinct main-character discoveries free. One optional, permanent Full Game Unlock, purchased inside the game, will open the remaining main-character collection. Failed mixtures and repeat discoveries will not use up the free discovery allowance.

There are no advertisements or subscriptions. In-game coins are earned through play and spent on hints; they are not sold for real money.

We do not integrate advertising networks, request advertising identifiers or use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs in the game. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-app advertising. We do not build advertising profiles or track your activity across other companies’ apps and websites.

3. Progress and settings stored on your device

The game stores information needed to continue your game, including:

  • Discovered characters and Little Finds, attempted mixtures, recipe history and earned achievements.
  • Your brew total, other gameplay statistics, in-game coin balance and hint progress.
  • A randomly generated save identifier and the information used to preserve your personal game outcome.
  • Sound, music, ambience, tutorial and other game preferences.
  • Full Game Unlock access status where the purchase feature is available.

These records are stored in the app’s local storage. They are not routinely uploaded to a Quiet Loop Games server. The random save identifier is used for your saved game, not advertising.

Clearing app data or deleting the app can remove local progress. Reinstalling a paid app does not necessarily restore your saved book. Device backups or files you exported may remain after the local copy is removed.

4. Backups and selected save files

Your operating system may back up or transfer app data according to your Apple, Google and device settings. Apple or Google operates that service; Quiet Loop Games does not operate a separate mobile cloud-save service.

If your version offers Export Save or Restore Save, these features use the system file picker to write or read a save file that you select. We do not upload that file to our servers. Choosing a cloud-storage location may send the file to the provider you select under that provider’s terms and privacy policy.

An exported save contains your game progress and random save identifier. Keep it in a location you trust, and delete unwanted copies separately from your app data. The file picker does not give the game general access to your other files.

5. Optional leaderboards and achievements

If your release offers platform leaderboards or achievements and you choose to connect, those features use Apple Game Center on iOS or Google Play Games on Android. Core gameplay does not require that sign-in.

The platform may process your player identifier, gaming profile, display name, avatar where available, cumulative “Most Brews” score and earned achievements to provide rankings and achievement records. Your leaderboard entry and gaming profile may be visible to other players according to the platform’s settings.

A repeat brew can increase your cumulative score; the score is not a record of your payment information. Platform leaderboard records are separate from your local saved game and are not a backup of your collections.

You can manage the connection, profile visibility and platform records through Apple or Google account and gaming-service controls. No platform score is submitted by a release in which the online feature is not enabled.

6. Optional Full Game Unlock and store information

Full Game Unlock is planned as a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase after closed testing. It is not enabled in the current closed-test builds. Where offered in a public release, Apple processes it on iOS and Google processes it on Android. Buying it is optional, and it does not require a Quiet Loop Games player account.

Where the purchase feature is enabled and you buy or restore the unlock, the store handles your store account, payment details, purchase history, transaction identifiers or purchase tokens, licensing and refund requests. The game uses the store’s purchase result and entitlement information to enable or restore full-game access. Quiet Loop Games does not receive your full payment-card or bank details. Stores may make sales, transaction and financial reports available to us as the developer.

Restoring a purchase restores eligible paid access through the relevant store account. It does not itself restore locally saved discoveries, hints or coins; those require your local game data or an available backup.

Apple, Google and your device software may also process service, security or diagnostic information under their own policies and your account or device settings. This is separate from an in-game advertising or analytics SDK.

7. Information you choose to send us

If you email us for support or a privacy request, we receive your email address and the contents and attachments you choose to send. For example, a save file or screenshot you send may contain game progress or other information visible in that file.

We use this information to respond, investigate the reported problem, handle privacy requests and maintain necessary support records. Please include only information needed for your request. Our email provider, Google Gmail, processes correspondence to deliver and store those messages.

8. Permissions, providers and disclosures

Core gameplay does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise location. Choosing an individual save file uses the system picker rather than broad file access. Optional platform services, store operations or opening an external policy page may require an internet connection.

The relevant providers process information under their own policies:

  • Apple Privacy Policy — App Store, Game Center and Apple device services.
  • Google Privacy Policy — Google Play, Play Games, Google device services and Gmail support correspondence.
  • GitHub Privacy Statement — hosting for this privacy-policy page.

Visiting this page may generate standard hosting logs, such as your IP address and browser information, handled by GitHub Pages. We have not added analytics scripts, advertising or tracking cookies to this page.

We may disclose information we hold where reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to a valid legal request, protect rights and security, or resolve misuse. We do not use support correspondence for advertising.

9. Retention, legal grounds and security

Local game records remain until removed or replaced on your device. You control exported copies and available device backups. Store, gaming-service and hosting records follow the relevant provider’s retention policies and account controls.

We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to resolve your request, keep necessary support records and meet legal obligations. When it is no longer needed for those purposes, we delete it or remove identifying information.

Where applicable data-protection law requires a legal basis, we process support information to provide requested assistance and for our legitimate interests in maintaining and securing the game and resolving inquiries. We rely on legal obligations where retention or disclosure is required by law, and on consent where consent is required for an optional feature. Providers explain the grounds for their own processing in their policies.

Providers may process information outside your country, including in the United States. Their policies describe relevant international-transfer safeguards. We use available device and service protections and limit access to information to the purposes described here. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

10. Your choices and privacy rights

  • Play core gameplay offline and without signing in to a platform gaming service.
  • Manage local app data, device backups and exported save files through the relevant device or storage controls.
  • Manage platform sign-in, profile visibility, purchase records and leaderboard data through Apple or Google.
  • Decide what information to include in a support message.

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of personal information, restrict or object to processing, and withdraw consent where processing relies on it. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

For requests concerning information held by Quiet Loop Games, contact quiteloopgames@gmail.com. We may request information reasonably necessary to verify and handle your request, and will respond as required by applicable law. We cannot remotely identify or delete a save held only on your device.

There is no Quiet Loop Games player account to delete. Deleting local app data does not delete records held independently by Apple, Google or a storage provider; use the relevant provider’s controls or contact us if you need help identifying them.

11. Children

The game does not ask players to provide their name, email address or other contact details to play, and has no player chat or user-content upload feature. It does not use behavioral advertising. Platform account and purchase features are subject to Apple’s and Google’s age and parental controls.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, contact us so we can investigate and remove it where appropriate.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game, its services or information practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. We will provide additional notice or obtain consent where applicable law requires it. The same policy URL covers iOS and Android.
