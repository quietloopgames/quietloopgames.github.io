1. About this policy

Quiet Loop Games ("we", "us" or "the developer") publishes Crop & Carry: Farm Tycoon ("the Game"). This policy explains how information is handled when you play the Game on iOS or Android, use optional advertising or purchase features, or contact us.

Features may differ by platform, release and region. Some builds work entirely offline and do not enable advertising or purchases. The advertising and purchase sections below apply when those services are included and used; they do not mean that every build includes every feature.

At a glance
  • Your farm progress and settings are saved on your device. Core farming does not require a game account.
  • Ad-enabled releases use Google AdMob. Advertising providers process certain device, interaction and diagnostic information, subject to applicable consent and privacy choices.
  • Optional purchases are processed by the App Store or Google Play. We do not receive your full card or bank details.
  • For privacy questions or data requests, email quiteloopgames@gmail.com.

2. Your saved farm and settings

The Game stores farm progress and preferences locally, including in-game money, upgrades, crops and inventory, workers, completed objectives, your chosen farmer, tutorial progress, and sound or graphics settings. It may keep a previous healthy save to help recover from a damaged checkpoint.

These local farm records are used to run the Game and restore your progress. We do not operate a mandatory game account or a developer cloud-save service for the mobile Game, and your farm is not automatically shared with other players. Device or operating-system backup behaviour depends on the platform, app configuration and your device settings.

You can start a new farm in the Game's settings. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its app data may permanently remove local progress. A local save does not itself provide cross-device recovery. Store purchase records are managed separately by Apple or Google.

3. Advertising and information used for ads

Ad-enabled releases may show banner ads, interstitial ads at breaks in play, and optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch for an in-game benefit. Google AdMob and advertising partners involved in delivering those ads may collect or receive:

  • Network and general location information: your IP address, which may be used to estimate an approximate location such as a country or region.
  • Device, app and permitted identifiers: device model, operating system, app and SDK information, advertising identifiers such as Android's advertising ID or Apple's IDFA where permitted, and other app/device or provider identifiers.
  • Interactions: app launches and activity associated with advertising, ad requests, impressions, taps, video views and rewarded-ad completion events.
  • Diagnostics: crash, error, performance and technical information used to understand app or advertising-service operation.
  • Privacy preferences: consent status, applicable privacy choices and related records needed to respect those choices.

These providers use information to deliver and measure ads, personalise ads where permitted, understand advertising performance, limit repeated ads, detect invalid traffic or fraud, and maintain security and service reliability. The information and advertising mode depend on the SDK configuration, region, consent and device settings. Advertising-service measurement does not mean that your complete saved farm is sent to an advertising provider.

Rewarded videos are optional. The Game may process the provider's completion result to grant the relevant benefit and prevent duplicate rewards. Choosing not to watch a rewarded video does not require you to provide contact or payment details.

4. Advertising consent, tracking and your choices

Where required, including relevant advertising-consent requirements in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland, ad-enabled releases present a consent message before requesting ads that require consent. Google’s consent-management services may be used to record and apply your choices.

Where a privacy-options entry point is required, you can reopen it through the Game's settings or its advertising-consent interface to review or change your choices. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal. Depending on applicable law and provider configuration, non-personalised or limited ads may be available; declining personalisation does not necessarily stop all technical processing needed for permitted advertising, security or fraud prevention.

On iOS: if a release requests permission to track activity across other companies' apps or websites, Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt is used. We do not access the IDFA or perform tracking that requires that permission without obtaining it. You can change the permission in your device's tracking settings.

On Android: you can use your device's advertising and privacy controls to reset or delete an advertising ID and manage available ad preferences. Available controls depend on your operating-system version.

We do not sell your saved farm or support correspondence. Some advertising-related disclosures may be considered a "sale", "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under certain US state privacy laws. Where those laws apply, you may exercise the corresponding opt-out rights through the advertising privacy controls, device controls or by contacting us about information we control.

5. Optional in-app purchases and advertising removal

Where purchases are offered, Apple processes App Store purchases on iOS and Google processes Google Play purchases on Android. Purchases may include an advertising-removal entitlement or other optional paid game features described on the relevant purchase screen.

The Game and any purchase-verification service used for that release may process a product identifier, transaction identifier or purchase token, purchase date, verification result and entitlement status to provide purchased features, restore eligible purchases, handle refunds or revocations, and prevent fraud. We do not receive your full payment card, bank details or store password from Apple or Google.

An advertising-removal purchase removes the advertising placements identified in its offer. Optional rewarded videos may remain available if the offer says so; their advertising-data processing applies only when the advertising service is used. Purchase availability and restoration depend on the platform and product type.

6. Support, diagnostics and platform services

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include, such as a description of a problem, device details, screenshots or diagnostic information. We use it to respond, investigate the issue and manage your request. Sending a support email is optional.

Copying support details inside the Game does not automatically send them to us. Please choose carefully what to include in an email. We do not need sensitive personal documents or full payment details to answer an ordinary gameplay question.

Apple, Google and their testing or distribution services may process installation, purchase, usage or crash information under their own privacy terms and your platform settings. If you use TestFlight on iOS, Apple's beta-testing data practices also apply. This policy does not give us access to your store password or all information in your Apple or Google account.

7. Purposes and legal grounds

We handle information to provide the Game, save progress, deliver and restore eligible purchases, provide optional advertising features, respect privacy choices, respond to support, protect the Game from fraud and comply with legal obligations.

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, including in the EEA and UK, the applicable basis may be performance of our agreement with you, your consent where required, compliance with a legal obligation, or our legitimate interests in supporting, securing and maintaining the Game where those interests are not overridden by your rights. Advertising partners explain their own processing purposes and legal bases in their notices and applicable consent messages.

Our service providers may process information in countries outside your country of residence, including the United States. Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, the relevant provider's arrangements may include adequacy decisions or recognised contractual safeguards. 10. Retention and deletion

  • Local farm data: remains on your device until you reset it, clear app data, uninstall the Game, or it is otherwise removed by your device. Platform backups, if any, have separate controls.
  • Support correspondence: kept while handling your request and only as long as reasonably needed for relevant follow-up, security, disputes or legal obligations.
  • Purchase and entitlement records: retained as needed to provide and restore eligible benefits, address refunds or fraud, and meet applicable obligations. Store records are also subject to Apple's or Google's retention rules.
  • Advertising, consent and diagnostic records: retained by the relevant service providers under their policies and applicable legal requirements. Records we control are retained only for the purposes described here and for as long as necessary for those purposes.

Retention depends on the type of record, the purpose, an ongoing request or dispute, and legal requirements. We delete or anonymise information when it is no longer needed, subject to applicable exceptions. We cannot remotely erase a farm stored only on your device, or delete records controlled independently by Apple, Google or another provider on your behalf without their required process.

11. Your rights and requests

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access or obtain a copy of personal information, correct it, request deletion, restrict or object to processing, obtain data portability, withdraw consent, or opt out of relevant advertising-related sharing. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority. Exercising applicable privacy rights will not result in unlawful discrimination.

For information controlled by Quiet Loop Games, email quiteloopgames@gmail.com with the subject "Crop & Carry privacy request". Describe the request and the information you believe we hold. We may ask for proportionate information to verify the request, but please do not send passwords, full payment details or unnecessary identity documents. We respond within the time required by applicable law.

The core mobile Game does not require a developer account to create or delete. For local farm data, use the Game or device controls described above. For store purchases, advertising-provider data or platform account information, use the relevant provider's privacy and account controls; we can explain which provider is relevant.

12. Children and family choices

The Game does not require players to provide their name, email address or other contact information to farm. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children where parental consent is legally required without that consent. We do not knowingly permit personalised advertising or tracking for children where prohibited by applicable law or platform rules.

Parents and guardians can use device and store parental controls to manage purchases, advertising permissions and access to online services. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us or that information has been handled improperly, contact us so we can investigate and delete information we control where required. Providers' age-related protections and applicable regional rules also apply to their services.

We may update this policy when the Game, its services or legal requirements change. The current policy will remain available at this address and the update date will change. Where required, we will provide an additional notice or obtain a new consent before materially different processing begins.
