1. Who we are and which games this covers

Quiet Loop Games ("we", "us" or "our") develops and publishes games. We are responsible for personal information we handle as described in this policy. Our privacy contact is quiteloopgames@gmail.com.

This policy applies to the current and future iOS and Android games published by Quiet Loop Games that identify this page as their privacy policy in the game, store listing or applicable privacy message. It also covers privacy requests and support correspondence about those games.

Coverage is based on the publisher and the link to this policy, rather than a list of individual game titles. It includes free games, advertising-supported games, paid games and games with optional purchases, to the extent their information practices are described below.

Read the sections for your version. A shared policy does not mean every game has advertisements, purchases, online accounts, leaderboards or cloud saves. The features available in your game and its in-app disclosures determine which sections apply. An app-specific notice may give additional details; any such notice must identify the game and explain the additional processing.

3. Progress, preferences and files on your device

Our games store the information needed to continue playing on your device. Depending on the game, this includes level or story progress, puzzle results, completed objectives, scores, gameplay statistics, virtual currency, inventory, crops, workers, collections, unlocked characters or cosmetics, vehicles, upgrades, tutorial state, and audio, language, graphics or accessibility settings.

A game may also store a random save identifier, recent healthy checkpoints, pending platform-score submissions, purchase-entitlement status, and records used to recover an interrupted action or avoid duplicate rewards. A random local save identifier is not, by itself, an advertising identifier.

Local storage is used to operate the game and remember your preferences. It is not automatically uploaded to Quiet Loop Games merely because you play. Online processing occurs through the enabled features described below or when you choose to send us information for support.

Where a game offers save export, import or restore from a selected file, it uses the file you choose through the system's file controls. An exported save can contain progress, preferences and a save identifier. Selecting a cloud-storage destination can send the file to the provider you choose, under that provider's terms. We do not receive that file unless you send it to us.

Clearing app data, resetting progress or deleting the app may remove local saves. Exported copies, operating-system backups, cloud saves, platform scores and store purchases are managed separately. A purchase restore restores eligible paid access, not necessarily your saved game.

4. Advertising and information used for ads

Our free games may be supported by advertising. Releases with advertising enabled may show banners, interstitial ads at eligible breaks, and optional rewarded videos. Google AdMob and the advertising partners involved in serving those ads may collect, receive or share:

  • Network and approximate location: IP address and a general location inferred from it, such as a country or region.
  • Device, app and permitted identifiers: device type, operating system, app/SDK versions, advertising identifiers such as Android's advertising ID or Apple's IDFA where permitted, app-set or other app/device identifiers, and applicable account-related identifiers processed by the provider.
  • Interactions and advertising records: app launches or interactions associated with advertising, ad requests, impressions, taps, video views and earned-reward events.
  • Technical information: error, crash, performance and other diagnostic information associated with the app and advertising SDK.
  • Privacy records: consent status and related preference records needed to apply your choices.

Providers use this information for ad delivery, personalization where permitted, measurement and advertising analytics, limiting repeated ads, security, invalid-traffic detection and fraud prevention. Processing depends on the release, SDK configuration, region, age-related treatment, device permissions and your privacy choices. Advertising measurement does not mean your complete local save is sent to an ad provider.

Rewarded ads are optional. The game uses the provider's earned-reward result to grant the advertised in-game benefit and prevent duplicate rewards. The benefit and availability are shown in the game. Declining a rewarded video does not authorize us to collect additional information.

An ad-removal or supporter purchase removes the placements described in that game's offer. Some games retain optional rewarded ads; others remove all ads or provide an alternative benefit. Data processing for any retained advertising applies when that service is used. A game sold without advertising does not become ad-supported because it links to this policy.

5. Advertising consent, tracking and your choices

Where required, including applicable requirements in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland, ad-enabled releases use an advertising-consent interface before processing that requires consent. Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) may obtain, store and communicate your choices to the relevant advertising providers.

Where a privacy-options entry point is required, you can revisit the available choices through the game's settings or advertising-privacy interface, commonly labelled Privacy Choices, Privacy Options or Ad Privacy. You can also contact us if you cannot locate the relevant control. Withdrawing consent affects subsequent processing based on it and does not make earlier lawful processing unlawful.

Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily prevent all ads or all technical processing. Non-personalized or limited ads may be available where permitted, with processing needed for their delivery, security, measurement or fraud prevention as described by the provider.

iOS: access to the IDFA or tracking that requires Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission is subject to that permission. You can manage it in your device's tracking settings.

Android: use your device's advertising and privacy settings to manage available ad controls, including resetting or deleting the advertising ID where supported.

US state choices: advertising-related disclosure or use of identifiers may be considered a sale, sharing or targeted advertising under some US state laws. Where applicable, you may exercise the relevant opt-out rights through available game/provider privacy controls or by contacting us about information we control. We do not sell support correspondence or local saved-game files to data brokers.

6. Paid games and optional in-app purchases

Some games are paid downloads; others offer optional purchases such as permanent game unlocks, ad removal, supporter upgrades, themes, characters, vehicle/content packs, hints or virtual currency. Only the products shown in your game's store or purchase interface are offered in that release.

Apple processes App Store payments on iOS and Google processes Google Play payments on Android. The game and relevant store may process product identifiers, transaction identifiers or purchase tokens, receipts or signed transaction information, purchase/verification status, purchase dates and entitlement records to deliver purchases, restore eligible benefits, handle refunds or revocations, and prevent fraud. Store sales and transaction reports may also be available to us as the developer.

We do not receive your full payment-card details, bank details or store password from Apple or Google. Store account records, payments, refunds and platform retention are governed by the relevant provider's terms and privacy policy.

Virtual coins, rewards and other in-game items are gameplay content. In simulated casino games, virtual currency has no cash value and cannot be withdrawn or exchanged for cash or real-world prizes. Restoring an eligible permanent purchase does not restore consumed virtual items or local progress unless the game provides a separate supported recovery feature.

7. Optional sign-in, leaderboards and achievements

Supported releases can connect to Apple Game Center on Apple devices or Google Play Games on Android. The platform may sign you in using your existing platform settings and permissions. Games with optional online features allow core play without requiring you to create a Quiet Loop Games player account.

These features may process a game-scoped or other permitted platform player identifier, gaming display name, avatar where provided, sign-in/availability state, scores and achievement progress. A local pending submission may associate a score with its player identifier so that it is submitted for the correct account. The data are used to authenticate the gaming session, show platform information, and record or display scores and achievements.

Your gaming profile and leaderboard entry, including nickname, avatar and score where supplied, can be visible to other players under the platform's rules and your applicable profile settings. Manage platform sign-in and activity sharing through Apple or Google. A score or achievement record is separate from a full game save.

Where a release offers Sign in with Apple for an account or progress feature, Apple may provide an app-related user identifier and, when authorized, a name and email address, including an Apple private-relay address. This information is used for the sign-in and progress feature you request. We do not receive your Apple password.

Any release that introduces a separate developer-operated account system or additional account data will identify that service and its data practices in this policy or a clearly linked app-specific notice, and provide the applicable account-deletion controls.

8. Optional cloud saves and device backups

Cloud progress is available only where your release offers it and the applicable connection, permission and account settings allow it. Apple services can include iCloud, GameKit Saved Games and CloudKit. Android releases with an enabled cloud feature can use Google Play Games Saved Games. A platform sign-in does not imply that a particular game has enabled cloud saving.

A cloud save can contain the progress and preferences described in section 3, game-specific inventory and statistics, a save identifier, and metadata such as schema/version, update time, play time or integrity information. The platform uses account-related identifiers to associate the save with the appropriate player. These records are used to synchronize or restore progress and resolve conflicting save versions.

Apple or Google hosts the platform save. A CloudKit private-database save is stored in the player's private iCloud database rather than on a public leaderboard. Cloud-save content is not made public merely because it is synchronized. These platform features do not, by themselves, give Quiet Loop Games your platform password or access to all your iCloud or Google Drive files.

The operating system can separately back up or transfer app data according to its settings. If you choose a storage provider when exporting a save, that provider receives the selected file under its own terms.

Turning off sync or signing out can stop future synchronization without deleting an existing cloud copy. Uninstalling an app or resetting its local progress does not necessarily delete cloud data. Use any cloud-deletion control provided by the game and the relevant Apple/Google data controls; contact us if you need help identifying the service. Cloud availability and compatibility depend on the game and platform.

9. Support, diagnostics and communications

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and anything you choose to attach. This can include your game/version, device information, screenshots, diagnostic output or an exported save. We use it to respond, investigate the problem, handle privacy requests and maintain necessary support records. Our email provider, Google Gmail, processes correspondence to deliver and store these messages.

Copying diagnostic details inside a game does not automatically send them to us. Please include only information needed for your inquiry and avoid passwords, full payment details or unrelated personal documents.

Apple, Google and enabled advertising/platform services may process installation, usage, crash, security or performance information under their own service policies and your settings. TestFlight and Google Play testing features have the relevant platform's testing and diagnostic practices. This is separate from sending us a support message.

Linking to this shared policy does not enable an additional analytics SDK. A release that introduces another analytics, crash-reporting or online-data provider will identify the provider, information and purpose in this policy or an app-specific notice before that additional processing begins.

10. Why information is used and legal grounds

Information is used to run and maintain the game, remember progress and preferences, provide requested platform and cloud features, deliver and restore purchases, provide permitted advertising, apply privacy choices, answer support requests, prevent fraud and misuse, and meet legal obligations.

Where applicable data-protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on performance of requested services where necessary, consent for processing that requires it, legal obligations, and legitimate interests in supporting and securing our games and responding to inquiries where those interests are not overridden by your rights. Providers explain the legal grounds for their own processing in their notices and, where relevant, the advertising-consent interface.

We do not use support correspondence or private saved-game content to make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects based solely on automated processing.

14. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access or obtain a copy of personal information, correct it, request deletion, restrict or object to processing, request portability, withdraw consent, or opt out of applicable advertising-related sale, sharing or targeted advertising. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority. We will not unlawfully discriminate against you for exercising applicable rights.

To make a request about information controlled by Quiet Loop Games, email quiteloopgames@gmail.com. Tell us the game, platform and nature of the request. We may request proportionate information needed to verify and handle it, and respond within the deadlines required by applicable law.

You can also manage available advertising preferences in the game and device, platform gaming profiles through Apple or Google, purchases through the relevant store, and local data/backups through your device or storage provider. For information independently controlled by a provider, that provider's privacy-request process applies.

15. Children, families and age limits

Intended audiences and age requirements differ by game. Follow the age information and parental controls in the game's store listing and in the game. Adult-only releases, including simulated casino games labelled 18+, are not directed to children.

We do not knowingly seek children's personal information without parental consent where required, or knowingly permit personalized advertising or tracking for children where prohibited by applicable law or platform rules. Age-related protections and parental controls of Apple, Google and any enabled provider also apply.

If a child-directed release needs to collect personal information or offer an online feature subject to parental permission, it must provide the applicable disclosures and permission flow before that processing. This shared policy does not replace those protections.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to us or that information has been handled improperly, contact us with the game and relevant details so we can investigate and remove information we control where required. You can use device and store controls to manage purchases and platform access.
