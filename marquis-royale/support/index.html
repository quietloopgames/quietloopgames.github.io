Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

Your coin balance, salon restorations, unlocked vaults, daily rewards and settings are saved locally on your device. Separate devices and browser/mobile installations do not automatically share the same save. Game Center scores are separate from local progress.

If progress appears missing, contact us before deleting the app or clearing app data, as these actions may erase the remaining local save. We do not operate a cloud-save service for this game.

A rewarded ad did not give me coins.

Coins are credited after the advertising provider confirms that the reward was earned. Closing an ad before the reward is earned or a failed ad does not grant coins. If you completed an ad but did not receive the displayed reward, email us with your platform, game version, approximate time and what you saw.

Why is an ad unavailable?

Advertising depends on your app version, internet connection, region, privacy choices and the provider's available inventory. Try again later with a stable connection. Rewarded ads are optional; an unavailable ad does not stop core gameplay.

How do I open Ruby Vault?

Restore the first three salon details. Each restoration requires its displayed coin cost and turn goal. Use Your Salon to see your next restoration and its requirements.

How does Biggest Turn work?

Biggest Turn records the largest coin return from a completed single turn, including its applicable cascade result. It is different from your total coin balance. Daily gifts and ad rewards do not count as spin wins.

Where enabled on iOS, Apple Game Center provides the online leaderboard. You can still play without signing in. Leaderboard availability depends on your platform and release.

I need help with a store purchase or refund.

Where purchases are offered, Apple or Google handles payment and receipts. Email us with the product name, your platform and store order reference so we can investigate delivery of the purchased item. Refund requests are handled through the store where the purchase was made.
